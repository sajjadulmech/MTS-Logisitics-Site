About Us

A Trusted Partner in Bangladesh & Global Freight Forwarding

MTS Logistics designs and operates multimodal supply chain solutions for businesses trading across Asia, the Middle East, Europe and the Americas.

Who We Are

Connecting Bangladesh to global trade

MTS Logistics Ltd. is a Bangladesh-based freight forwarder and multimodal logistics provider, positioned at the crossroads of the Maritime Silk Route and the Trans-Pacific trade corridor. We plan and execute sea freight, air freight, customs clearance, warehousing and project logistics for businesses moving cargo between Bangladesh and the world’s major markets.

Our approach is built on operational discipline, transparent communication and accountability at every stage of the shipment lifecycle. That consistency is what allows clients to rely on us for a single service or a fully managed, end-to-end supply chain.
