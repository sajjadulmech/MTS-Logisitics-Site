Quick Reference

Basic Incoterms at a Glance

A quick overview of the 11 Incoterms 2020 rules.

EXW

Ex Works

Seller makes goods available at their premises; the buyer handles all transport and clearance.

All Transport Modes

FCA

Free Carrier

Seller delivers goods, cleared for export, to a carrier nominated by the buyer.

All Transport Modes

CPT

Carriage Paid To

Seller pays carriage to the named destination; risk passes at the first carrier.

All Transport Modes

CIP

Carriage and Insurance Paid To

Like CPT, but the seller also arranges insurance covering the goods to destination.

All Transport Modes

DAP

Delivered at Place

Seller delivers when goods reach the named place, ready for unloading by the buyer.

All Transport Modes

DPU

Delivered at Place Unloaded

Seller delivers and unloads the goods at the named place of destination.

All Transport Modes

DDP

Delivered Duty Paid

Seller delivers goods cleared for import, bearing all costs and duties to destination.

All Transport Modes

FAS

Free Alongside Ship

Seller delivers goods alongside the vessel at the named port of shipment.

Sea & Inland Waterway

FOB

Free On Board

Seller delivers goods on board the vessel; risk passes once they are loaded.

Sea & Inland Waterway

CFR

Cost and Freight

Seller pays cost and freight to the destination port; risk passes on loading.

Sea & Inland Waterway

CIF

Cost, Insurance and Freight

Like CFR, plus the seller provides marine insurance to the destination port.

Sea & Inland Waterway
