Ocean Transportation

Sea Freight

As an FMC-licensed ocean freight forwarder in Bangladesh, MTS Logistics coordinates frequent container sailings connecting Chattogram Port (Chittagong) and Mongla Port to major international trade lanes across North America, Europe, Asia, and the Middle East.

We manage Full Container Load (FCL), Less than Container Load (LCL), Garments on Hanger (GOH), and temperature-controlled reefer cargo — backed by dependable carrier space allocation, feeder vessel coordination via Colombo and Singapore, and seamless customs documentation.

Multimodal

Sea-Air Shipment

Reach any global destination in under 20 days — a strategic multimodal solution that overcomes Bangladesh production delays and enables Just-In-Time retail deliveries at significantly lower cost than direct air freight.

Combining ocean transit from Chattogram to major transshipment hubs (Dubai, Colombo, Singapore) with priority air cargo connections to Europe and the Americas, managed under a single through bill of lading.

Air Cargo

Air Freight

Direct air cargo operations from Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC) in Dhaka with competitive rates and confirmed allocations on preferred international passenger and freighter airlines.

From urgent fashion apparel samples and high-value textiles to pharmaceuticals and perishables, our Dhaka air freight team manages space booking, airport transfer, customs inspection, and prompt uplift worldwide.

Compliance

Customs Clearance

Licensed customs brokerage and regulatory compliance across Bangladesh’s major ports of entry: Dhaka Airport Customs House (DAC), Chattogram Customs House, Kamalapur ICD, off-dock container freight stations (CFS), and Export Processing Zones (DEPZ, CEPZ).

Our licensed customs team coordinates classification, appraisement, HS code verification, duty assessment, and import/export documentation to keep shipments moving without unnecessary delay.

Warehousing & Trucking

Origin warehousing and buyer consolidation for Bangladesh export industries. Shipments prepared to exact buyer Distribution Centre (DC) specifications with inventory control, palletization, barcoding, and sorting.

Inter-district door-to-door trucking across the industrial belts of Dhaka, Gazipur, Narayanganj, and the Chattogram port transport corridor for both FCL and LCL consignments.
