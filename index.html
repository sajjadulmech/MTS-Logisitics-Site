About MTS

International Freight Forwarding & Logistics from Bangladesh

MTS Logistics is a Bangladesh-based freight forwarder connecting businesses to markets across Asia, the Middle East, Europe and the Americas. We deliver sea freight, air freight, customs brokerage, warehousing and project logistics, supported by our own offices and a trusted international partner network. Every shipment is planned around what your supply chain actually needs, giving you the visibility and reliability modern trade demands — from a single movement to full end-to-end management.
